Data Classifications & Policy

Classification Overview

Data classifications are metadata labels applied to Fabric items to document sensitivity levels. They guide data handling practices and access governance. Classifications are for documentation only and do not automatically enforce access control in Fabric.

Classification Policy

  • PII (Personally Identifiable Information): Contains names, emails, SSNs, or customer identifiers. Restricted access; data retention policies apply.
  • Confidential: Internal business data; restricted to domain teams. Sensitive metrics, financial data, or competitive information.
  • Public: No sensitive data. Can be shared externally. Suitable for dashboards and reports.
